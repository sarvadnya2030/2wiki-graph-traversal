KEY FINDINGS (FOR COURSE SUBMISSION)
================================================================================

PRIMARY FINDING:
  Graph construction is more important than algorithm choice.

EVIDENCE:
  - Dijkstra (simple) consistently beats all competitors: F1@10 = 0.237-0.256
  - Semantic methods fail despite better embeddings: F1 drops from 0.031→0.021
  - Hub pruning & PPMI reweighting don't help: PHP F1 = 0.188 (-24%)
  - Extended hops hurt: 4-5 hops worse than 3-hops
  
CONCLUSION:
  On co-occurrence graphs, Dijkstra dominates because it:
  1. Respects edge weights directly (no probability spreading)
  2. Implicitly avoids hubs (low-frequency edges prioritized)
  3. Needs no semantic signals (structural signals sufficient)
  
  Semantic methods fail because:
  1. Weak semantic signal on co-occurrence graph
  2. Aggressive filtering breaks sparse graph connectivity
  3. Better embeddings paradoxically make things worse

IMPLICATION:
  For multi-hop QA: Invest in graph quality (typed relations, semantic weights)
  rather than algorithmic sophistication.
